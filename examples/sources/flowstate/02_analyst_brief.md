## DOCUMENT 2: ANALYST BRIEF EXCERPT

*From "Workflow Automation Platforms: Market Outlook 2026" — Forrester research summary, internal extract*

The workflow automation category continues to expand at a 22% CAGR, but the market is bifurcating. Horizontal platforms targeting general operations workflows face increasing pressure from two directions. Vertical-specific players are winning mid-market and enterprise deals by offering pre-built workflows, compliance accelerators, and industry-native integrations. At the same time, incumbents in adjacent categories such as ERP, CRM, and ITSM are bundling lightweight workflow tools into existing contracts, compressing SMB demand for standalone platforms.

Buyer behavior has shifted notably in mid-market and enterprise segments. In 2022, 64% of workflow automation buyers reported "ease of setup" as a top-three selection criterion. In 2025, that figure is 38%. The new top criteria are integration depth (71%), governance and audit capability (68%), and vendor stability (54%). Buyers in regulated industries, including financial services, healthcare, and insurance, are increasingly unwilling to consider horizontal platforms unless they offer dedicated vertical accelerators or strong partnerships with vertical specialists.

Forrester estimates that 31% of enterprise workflow automation spend in 2026 will go to vertical-specific platforms, up from 12% in 2023. Horizontal platforms that do not establish vertical credibility risk being relegated to SMB and departmental use cases, where pricing power is limited and competition from bundled offerings is intensifying.
