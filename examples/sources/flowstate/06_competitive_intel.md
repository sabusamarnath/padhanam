*Prepared by Product Marketing, September*

**Veritone Flow** — Founded 2020. Vertical play in financial services. Pre-built workflow library for KYC, AML refresh, vendor risk, regulatory reporting. Native integrations with Fenergo, Actimize, NICE. Raised $80M Series B in March. List price 40 to 60% higher than Flowstate per seat. Won approximately 14 deals against us in the last twelve months, primarily in banking and asset management. Their pitch: "We are not a workflow platform. We are a regulated financial services operating system."

**Sapience Ops** — Healthcare-specific. HIPAA-native architecture, EHR integrations, clinical workflow templates. Less direct overlap, but increasingly showing up in deals where the buyer is consolidating clinical and administrative workflows.

**Lattice Banking Workflows** — Smaller, regional bank focus. Less of a threat in enterprise but eating into our mid-market financial services pipeline.

**Horizontal incumbents bundling workflow** — Salesforce Flow, ServiceNow App Engine, Microsoft Power Automate. Compressing demand at the SMB tier, particularly where the buyer already owns one of these platforms.

**Internal observation:** Flowstate has historically positioned on flexibility and time to value. Both are weakening as differentiators. Flexibility is now interpreted by enterprise buyers as "we will have to build everything ourselves." Time to value, our original wedge, is now matched or beaten by vertical specialists who ship pre-built solutions.

## INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE EXERCISE

Work through the seven steps in order. Resist the temptation to jump ahead.

1. **Define the problem.** Write a single problem statement that is specific, measurable, action-oriented, relevant, and time-bound. The CEO memo offers one framing. The documents may support a different framing.

2. **Disaggregate.** Build an issue tree that breaks the problem into mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive branches. Force yourself to go at least three levels deep on the branches that look most material.

3. **Prioritize.** Decide which branches are worth your time. Use a 2x2 of impact versus ability to influence, or a similar lens. Be ruthless. The pack contains red herrings.

4. **Workplan.** For each prioritized branch, specify the analysis needed, the data source, the hypothesis being tested, and the decision the analysis enables.

5. **Analyze.** Work through the data in the pack. Note where data is missing and what you would request.

6. **Synthesize.** Write the answer in one paragraph. Then build a pyramid: governing thought, three supporting arguments, evidence beneath each.

7. **Communicate.** Draft the executive recommendation. What is the bet? What are the trade-offs? What happens if you are wrong?
