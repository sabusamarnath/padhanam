## DOCUMENT 3: CUSTOMER INTERVIEW NOTES

### Interview A: Sarah Okafor, Head of Operations, Brightline Logistics
*Customer since 2022. Mid-market. 180 seats. Renewed in August at 22% expansion.*

**Q: Walk me through how Flowstate fits into your stack today.**
We use it across operations, finance, and people ops. I think we have about forty live workflows. Onboarding new hires, invoice approvals, shipment exception handling, contract renewals. It is genuinely the backbone of how we run.

**Q: What made you pick Flowstate originally?**
Honestly, I built the first three workflows myself in a weekend. I had been burned by enterprise tools that needed a six-month implementation. Flowstate let me prove value before I had to ask anyone for budget.

**Q: Anything frustrating?**
The reporting is weak. We export to a BI tool. And every time we ask about SOC reporting or audit trails our CSM goes quiet for two weeks then comes back with a workaround. We are not regulated so it does not block us, but our parent company is going public and we are about to inherit a lot of compliance requirements. That is going to be a problem.

### Interview B: David Mehta, VP Digital Transformation, Cordillera Capital
*Churned September. Enterprise. 1,400 seats at peak. $340K ACV.*

**Q: What led to the decision to leave?**
A combination of things. The honest answer is we never got to scale. We bought Flowstate eighteen months ago to standardize workflows across our portfolio companies. The vision was beautiful. The execution was painful.

**Q: Painful how?**
Time to value. Every new workflow needed someone who understood Flowstate deeply, and that person became a bottleneck. We tried to train champions in each portfolio company. It worked for the simple stuff. For anything involving our compliance reviewers or our regulated entities, we hit walls. Flowstate is a great tool. It is not a platform for an organization like ours.

**Q: What did you move to?**
Veritone Flow for the regulated entities. They had pre-built workflows for our exact use cases. KYC refresh, vendor risk assessment, regulatory filing prep. We did not have to build, we had to configure. That changed the math entirely.

**Q: Was it a price decision?**
Veritone is more expensive per seat. It was not a price decision. It was a "we will actually get there" decision.

### Interview C: Priya Sundaram, IT Director, Westmark Health Services
*Stalled deal. Pipeline value $480K. In late-stage evaluation for four months.*

**Q: Where are you with the evaluation?**
Honestly, we have stalled. Your team is great. The product demos well. But every time we get into a technical deep dive with our security and compliance teams, something comes up that we have to escalate to your team, who then comes back with a roadmap commitment rather than a current capability.

**Q: Can you give me a specific example?**
HIPAA audit logging. Role-based access at the workflow step level. Data residency in Canada. None of these are exotic asks for a healthcare buyer. We were told they are on the roadmap. Our CISO is not going to sign off on a tool based on a roadmap.

**Q: What happens next?**
We are looking at a healthcare-specific workflow vendor. We did not want to. We liked the breadth of Flowstate. But we cannot wait.
