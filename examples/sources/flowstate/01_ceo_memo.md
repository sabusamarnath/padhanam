## DOCUMENT 1: CEO MEMO TO LEADERSHIP TEAM

**From:** Nestor Chen, CEO
**To:** Leadership team
**Date:** October 6
**Subject:** Q3 results and what comes next

Team,

We closed Q3 at $26.8M ARR against a plan of $29.4M. New ARR came in at $1.9M versus a $3.1M target. This is the second quarter in a row we have missed, and the board is paying close attention.

The narrative we have been telling ourselves is that this is a sales execution problem. Pipeline coverage was 3.2x at the start of the quarter, which should have been enough. Win rates dropped from 28% to 19%. Average deal size is up 34% year over year, which is consistent with our upmarket strategy, but cycle times have stretched and too many deals are slipping or dying late stage.

I want to be honest. I am not certain the sales execution narrative is complete. A few things are bothering me:

1. Our SMB segment, which was 70% of new logos last year, is now 41% and trending down. We assumed this was healthy substitution as we moved upmarket. I am no longer sure.

2. Customer success flagged that expansion revenue in our 2022 and early 2023 cohorts is softening. Net revenue retention dropped from 118% to 104% over the last three quarters.

3. Two of our largest pipeline losses this quarter went to Veritone Flow, a competitor I had barely heard of eighteen months ago. Both deals were in financial services.

The Series C thesis was that we would ride workflow automation tailwinds into mid-market and enterprise, defending the SMB base while shifting product and GTM upward. The thesis may still be right. The execution may not be.

I am asking the PM team to lead a structured problem-solving exercise. I want a clear diagnosis and a recommendation by November 1. I do not want a list of forty things we could do. I want to know what is actually wrong and what we should bet on.

Nestor
