## DOCUMENT 4: CUSTOMER AND SUPPLY-SIDE RESEARCH SUMMARY

*Compiled from 22 buyer interviews and 17 supplier interviews conducted in September and October.*

### Buyer findings

The dominant theme across buyer interviews is post-match friction. 67% of interviewed buyers report meaningful difficulty managing the engagement after a supplier is selected. Document exchange, milestone tracking, scope changes, and invoicing happen outside Forgepath. Buyers describe the platform as "useful for finding people, useless for working with them."

When asked what would most increase their likelihood of returning to Forgepath for their next sourcing need, buyers cited the following, ranked:

1. More confidence in supplier quality and reliability (named by 18 of 22)
2. Tools to manage the project once engaged (named by 15 of 22)
3. Better matching speed and precision (named by 9 of 22)
4. Lower platform fees (named by 4 of 22)
5. More supplier choice in their category (named by 3 of 22)

12 of 22 buyers mentioned that they have used Forgepath once but used external channels for subsequent sourcing. The most common reason cited was a poor first experience with a Forgepath-vetted supplier whose work did not meet expectations.

When asked about adjacent service needs, buyers mentioned: regulatory consulting (6), supply chain design (5), industrial automation (4), specialized recruiting (3), embedded financing (2). No buyer named aerospace or defense services as a need from Forgepath.

### Supplier findings

Suppliers report two structural issues. First, they receive a high volume of low-quality briefs. 42% of inbound briefs are described as too vague to bid against. Suppliers spend significant time qualifying briefs before they can quote, which suppresses participation in the platform.

Second, suppliers describe supplier acquisition as inconsistent. Suppliers who joined through outbound recruiting report a thorough onboarding experience. Suppliers who joined through inbound channels report being on the platform for months before receiving meaningful inbound demand, and several have churned as a result.

13 of 17 suppliers indicated that a tiered certification or quality designation, with associated visibility benefits on the platform, would increase their engagement and willingness to invest in Forgepath as a channel.

On adjacent categories, 9 suppliers reported they could serve aerospace and defense work if the platform supported the compliance overhead. 4 suppliers reported European operations and noted they would welcome European demand.
