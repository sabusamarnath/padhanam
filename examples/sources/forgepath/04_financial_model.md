## DOCUMENT 6: FINANCE AND RESOURCE SNAPSHOT

*From CFO Pre-read for Strategic Offsite.*

The Series C extends our cash runway to approximately 32 months at current burn. The board has approved an increase in annual operating expense from $28M to $44M over the planning period, with the explicit expectation that this investment translates into durable revenue growth and improving unit economics.

The leadership team should plan against the following annualized investment envelope:

- Engineering and product capacity: room to grow from 42 to approximately 75 people. Realistic capacity to ship two major product bets concurrently. Three is a stretch. Four is not credible.
- Go-to-market capacity: room to grow from 28 to approximately 50 people. Sufficient for one major geographic or segment expansion concurrent with continued core market motion.
- Operations and supply: room to grow from 18 to approximately 35 people. Sufficient to materially upgrade supplier quality infrastructure in current categories, or to stand up a new category from zero, but not both at scale.
- Marketing and brand: room to grow from 11 to approximately 22 people. Sufficient for sustained category-building investment in current segments, not sufficient for parallel brand investment in new segments or geographies.

The CFO has flagged that any embedded financing or balance-sheet-intensive adjacency would require either separate capital or a partnership structure. It cannot be funded out of the current operating envelope without material trade-offs.

The implication is that the strategic plan must select. Not every bet on the table can be funded. The leadership team should expect to make explicit cuts at the offsite.
