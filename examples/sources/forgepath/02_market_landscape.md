## DOCUMENT 5: MARKET AND COMPETITIVE SNAPSHOT

*Prepared by Corporate Development, October.*

### Market sizing

Total addressable market for specialized industrial services in North American mid-market manufacturing is estimated at $182B annually. Forgepath's served available market today, defined as the three core categories with active liquidity, is approximately $34B. Forgepath's current GMV of $180M represents 0.5% penetration of served available market.

Geographic concentration of mid-market manufacturers is heaviest in the Midwest (32%), Texas and Gulf region (18%), Southeast (15%), Northeast (14%), West Coast (11%), Mountain and Plains (10%). Forgepath's current customer concentration mirrors this distribution with one exception: the Southeast is underpenetrated relative to its share of the manufacturer base.

Aerospace and defense services represents an additional $42B North American TAM. Compliance and credential barriers, including AS9100, ITAR, and DFARS, materially limit the addressable supply base. Estimated time to establish a credible aerospace supply panel is 18 to 24 months.

European industrial services TAM is estimated at $96B. Cross-border supplier engagement carries regulatory, tax, and operational complexity that has historically limited horizontal marketplace expansion.

### Competitive context

There is no direct horizontal competitor at scale in mid-market industrial services in North America. The category is fragmented across specialist consulting firms, regional service providers, and incumbent buyer-supplier relationships. Forgepath's primary competition is buyer inertia and existing vendor relationships, not other platforms.

Two adjacent platforms warrant monitoring:

- Mfg.com and Xometry operate in adjacent manufacturing categories (production parts, prototyping) and have shown limited but real expansion into services adjacencies.
- A new entrant, Verkstad, has raised $18M and is targeting European industrial services with a regional focus. Not currently competitive in North America.

The strategic risk is not a competitor displacing Forgepath in its current niche. The strategic risk is Forgepath failing to compound its current position before a well-capitalized horizontal entrant or a vertical incumbent expands into the category.
