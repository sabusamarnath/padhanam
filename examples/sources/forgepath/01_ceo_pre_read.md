## DOCUMENT 1: CEO OFFSITE PRE-READ

**From:** Anika Rao, CEO and Co-founder
**To:** Leadership team
**Date:** November 4
**Subject:** Where Forgepath is going next: pre-read for the strategy offsite

Team,

We closed the Series C on the strength of a story. Industrial services in the mid-market is a $180B sourcing problem that runs on email, spreadsheets, and personal networks. Forgepath has shown that a marketplace can work in this space. GMV grew 78% last year and we are tracking 41% this year. We have 380 active buyers and 2,400 vetted suppliers. We are the only platform in the category with meaningful liquidity in three of our top five service lines.

The question I want us to answer at the offsite is what Forgepath becomes by 2029.

My working draft of the vision: *Forgepath becomes the operating system for industrial services. Any manufacturer, anywhere, finding and managing any specialized service they need, on a single platform that they trust more than their existing vendor relationships.*

I know this is broad. I want it to be broad. I do not want us to constrain ourselves by current product surface or current category mix. The Series C thesis was that we earn the right to expand, and I want the next three years to be about earning that right aggressively.

A few open questions I want the team to come to the offsite ready to debate:

1. Are we a marketplace, a software platform, or a fintech-enabled services infrastructure company? Each path implies different investments.

2. Should we deepen in our current three core categories or expand to adjacent categories where we have weaker but real demand signal?

3. What is the right geographic posture? We are North America focused today. Several large customers have asked about European supplier access.

4. How do we think about the role of embedded financing? I have been talking to a few fintech founders about partnering on receivables financing for our suppliers. I think there is something here.

I am asking each function lead to circulate a memo before the offsite outlining what they think the most leveraged bet is for Forgepath in the next 36 months. Bring evidence.

Anika
