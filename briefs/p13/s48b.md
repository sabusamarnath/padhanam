# P13 S48b — Intent-classification evaluation substrate

Thread 2 of the parallel post-S47 forward path. Two smoke arcs across
S46 and S47 surfaced model-quality findings (intent-classification
reliability at qwen2.5:7b; latency viability at qwen2.5:14b) from
integration smokes that were not built for component-quality
evaluation. The diagnostic forwarded from the post-S47 strategic
conversation: integration smokes are wrong methodology for component-
quality questions; component questions need dedicated evaluation
surfaces; full-stack smokes verify integration, not component
quality. The architecture's port-and-adapter shape exists specifically
to make component testing possible in isolation, and the smokes have
been asked to do work the architecture is already structurally
designed to split.

The intent-classification evaluation substrate is the missing
component-quality surface. P11's retrieval-evaluation substrate at
`contexts/retrieval_evaluation/` is the structural precedent (gold
set; evaluation runner; metric calculator; per-tenant audit-chain
integration; CLI sub-app for invocation). S48b builds the analogous
substrate at `contexts/intent_classification_evaluation/`.
