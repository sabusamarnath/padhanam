## S44b pre-write reconciliation outcome (build-session annotation)

This section is appended at the S44b build session per the briefs/ living-record discipline; it records the operator-confirmed reshape before commit 1. The brief above is preserved as drafted at framing; the reshape below governs the build.

The seven-surface (plus one operator-added eighth) pre-write reconciliation surfaced three structural-honesty findings and four mechanical corrections. Operator-confirmed dispositions:

- **Finding 1 — cross-context import contract.** The brief's orchestrations in `contexts/intake/application/` directly importing `contexts/portfolio/application/` use cases violate the import-linter "Cross-context: application layers are independent" contract (D16/D17/D28); AC 13's claim was the inverse of the rule. **Disposition: consumer-port pattern.** The orchestrations stay in `contexts/intake/application/` but depend on a consumer-defined `PortfolioWriter` Protocol at `contexts/intake/application/ports/portfolio_writer.py`; the `apps/` composition layer provides the implementation that invokes `contexts.portfolio.application` use cases directly (legal for `apps/`). Precedent: `contexts/agent/application/ports/run_history_writer.py`. D127 alternative (e) lands explicitly.
- **Finding 2 — `create_data_point` write-surface gap.** The brief made `Assertion.intake_id` required-at-domain-level but gave `create_data_point` no intake path. **Disposition: 2a.** A third orchestration `record_intake_and_create_data_point` plus a POST `/api/v1/data_points` route plus a CLI `create-data-point` update. Three orchestrations of the same pattern stay at `contexts/intake/`; the `contexts/orchestration/` trigger is a distinct architectural concern, not an instance count. `intake_id` is nullable at the domain and persistence layers. `apps/api/_portfolio_dto.py` splits: read DTOs stay; the write DTOs (CreateCase, CreateDataPoint, ReviseDataPoint shapes) move to a new `apps/api/_portfolio_write_dto.py`.
- **Finding 3 — transaction semantics.** A single transaction across the intake and portfolio adapters is not available without a unit-of-work refactor. **Disposition: two-transaction intake-first ordering.** The IntakeRecord writes first; on downstream failure it persists as the canonical record-of-attempt — structurally honest for the audit-trail-integrity argument. D128 drops the "single transaction" claim. The unit-of-work refactor is forwarded to `charter/phase-2-audit-inputs.md` consideration.
- **Finding 4 — Assertion file path.** Assertion lives at `contexts/portfolio/domain/assertion.py`, not `data_point.py`.
- **Finding 5 — `_agent_runtime_wiring.py` at 912 lines.** Past the brief's own 600-line split trigger; second-instance file-topology-budget evidence. S44b's wiring still lands there; the split is forwarded to `charter/phase-2-audit-inputs.md`.
- **Finding 6 — migration revision-string length.** `0018_intake_id_on_cases_and_assertions` is 38 chars, over alembic's 32-char ceiling; shortened to `0018_intake_id_columns`.
- **Finding 7 — D128 scope.** D128 scopes honestly to "the platform's write surfaces" with portfolio as the first implementer.
- **Surface 8 (operator-added) — cross-context-contract verification** is added as a standing pre-write reconciliation surface from S44b forward.
