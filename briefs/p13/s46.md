## As-built pre-write reconciliation outcome (S46)

Nine surfaces verified. Surfaces 1–2, 4–5, 7 absorbed mechanically. Four structural findings surfaced and were operator-approved before code:

- **Finding A (Surface 9 + D122 conflict).** Four non-test call sites to `complete`/`stream_complete`. The brief's Path B (required tier parameter) contradicts D122's committed "Phase 1 call sites preserve current behaviour with opt-in retrofit." Disposition: **Path A** — `latency_tier: LatencyTier = LatencyTier.REAL_TIME_REQUIRED` defaulted parameter.
- **Finding B (cell layer placement).** A cell holding ports cannot sit at the pure-domain layer per the `layers-messaging` hexagonal contract. Disposition: cell moves to `contexts/messaging/application/manual_entry_cell.py`; intent value objects stay at `contexts/messaging/domain/intent.py`.
- **Finding C (ModelIdentifier at call signature).** Call sites carry no Provider/Account knowledge; the Configuration layer overlaps existing request fields. Disposition: `ModelIdentifier` composes at the LiteLLM adapter boundary; the public signature grows a `latency_tier` parameter; composition lands at a sibling `contexts/inference/adapters/outbound/litellm/model_ontology.py` to keep `adapter.py` under the 800-line ceiling.
- **Finding D (Surface 8 — consumer port shape).** `resolve_target` needs portfolio read access the brief's write-only framing omitted. Disposition: a single `PortfolioGateway` consumer port carrying both the resolution reads and the three orchestration writes; messaging-owned DTOs; one apps/-layer adapter.

Autonomous dispositions: the webhook **augments** (not replaces) `record_intake_and_record_inbound_message`; `cited_audit_events` stays empty at S46 (intake-owned write-result DTOs do not surface audit-event IDs); the StructuredOutputPort method is `generate_structured` (brief said `complete_structured`).
