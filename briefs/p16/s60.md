### New decision (confirm the index is the next free number)

> **D159 — Calendar-sourced items in the daily-driver Today surface, and the in-product Connections page (calendar one-tap).**
>
> The Today surface renders real calendar events alongside OPEN Cases and Commitments, sourced through a calendar consumer-port reader into `build_today_view`, on the same D17 cross-context read seam as `OpenCasesReader`. Calendar events carry tier and category via a calendar-to-domain mapping fixed at connection time: a connected calendar holds a domain tag (work, personal, family), and its events inherit it. The served surface implements the design-language Today surface (design-language §1–3) against `charter/brand/tokens.css`, with the three-channel identity rule (icon for category, initials for named context, colour for tier) and the shared status-pill vocabulary. The item drawer carries the S59 conversation cell (D158); for a calendar item the cell is the `calendar_conversation` implementer reached over the same HTTP-cell path, so calendar items are conversational, not view-only. The Connections page implements design-language §9: Google one-tap calendar connect with a read-only badge and a calendars-to-include manage panel; intake is `calendar.readonly` (D148).
>
> This is the first daily-driver surface rendering external-source items in the today list and the first in-product Connections surface. It is the Phase 2-A dogfooding entry point: the surface the week-of-use restraint gate (D156) runs against on real data. Email-into-list, the week matrix, the dashboard, the journal, and the Map/Flow graph views are out of scope and named below.

The minimal form for this slice: the calendar's domain tag is set when the calendar is included, and events inherit it. Record in the S60 marker whether this needs a clause added to `design-language.md §9`, and address it in reflection prompt 1.
