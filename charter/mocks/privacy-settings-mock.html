Padhanam/Settings · Privacy

Privacy and data

Your work and personal life live in one graph, and that graph is sensitive. You own it. These controls govern where it sits, what leaves it, and how completely it can be erased.
Where your data lives
Your tenant has its own dedicated database, isolated from every other customer. Choose the region it is stored in.
Encryption On
Everything is encrypted at rest. Your personal causal graph, the wiring between health, personal life, and work, is held in isolation for your tenant and encrypted to the same standard as the rest of your data.
Sensitive data boundary
You decide what counts as sensitive. Anything you mark is kept on the local model and never leaves your tenant, even when you use a hosted model for work.
Connected accounts
Imported calendar events and mail become items. Choose how long the original is kept after import. Manage connections →
Reasoning traces
Padhanam records how it reasoned so you can audit any recommendation it makes. Choose how long those traces are kept.
Traces are included in a full wipe below, so erasing your data erases how the assistant reasoned about it too.
Your data is yours
Normal edits never silently erase your originals, so the record stays auditable. When you want to leave, you can take everything or remove everything.
Export everythingAll items, workspaces, the causal graph, and your journal, as a portable file.
Delete all my dataA clean wipe across every store. This cannot be undone.

This removes your data from every store: your database, the causal graph, and all reasoning traces. It is immediate and cannot be undone.
