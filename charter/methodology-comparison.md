## Preamble

Padhanam's discipline sits among a growing landscape of methodologies for AI-assisted code. Karpathy named vibe coding in February 2025 as the baseline. Brian Madison's BMAD-METHOD, the TÂCHES Get Shit Done framework, GitHub Spec Kit, AWS Kiro, and Trey Research AI/works with its 3-3-3 delivery model each add structure for different actor counts and different scopes of work. Comparing the architect-implementer pattern across the same dimensions surfaces what is structurally distinctive about Padhanam's discipline, what is current-scope-limited, and where the methodology has named forward commitments or forward hypotheses that are not yet evidence-bearing.

## Close

The TBD cell on reverse engineering is honest: Padhanam's methodology has only been exercised forward-engineering and has not earned a claim about legacy work. The throughput-claim cell carries a one-phase deferral; the maintenance cell carries an explicit forward commitment; the multi-implementer cell carries a forward hypothesis to be tested when evidence accumulates. The comparison reads truthfully against the bet's claim that what travels is the methodology, not the platform: what is true today is what the methodology document at Phase 1 close articulates; what is committed forward and what is hypothesised forward both live in the deferred-decisions log, in their respective postures.

## Accuracy flags for verification

The verifier at Phase 2 close should resolve these before any cell is published to the deck.

1. **GitHub Spec Kit, reverse-engineering cell.** Genuine source conflict. GitHub's marketing claims brownfield modernisation; the primary `spec-driven.md` document states spec-driven development does not explicitly address legacy codebases. The cell currently states the conflict rather than picking a side.
2. **Trey Research 3-3-3, process-shape cell.** Sources conflict on whether "3-3-3" denotes 3 days / 3 weeks / 3 months or simply "90 days total." The cell currently says "Published phase timings vary across sources" and asserts neither. Resolve against a Trey Research primary source.
3. **AWS Kiro naming.** Amazon brands the product "Kiro" (built on AWS and Bedrock, not a formal AWS service line). The preamble and the row label use "AWS Kiro" per the operator's preamble text. Confirm or change to "Kiro."
4. **BMAD-METHOD creator attribution.** The preamble names the creator as "Brian Madison." This is operator-supplied text and was not independently verified; the repository is `bmad-code-org`. Verify the name before public release.
5. **Padhanam decision-audit cell, entry count.** The cell says "Ninety-plus entries" to match the deck's existing language. The accurate count at 2026-05-20 is roughly 113 (D113 is the latest). Refresh all decision-count figures across the deck together at integration.
