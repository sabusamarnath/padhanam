# Roadmap

The living strategic-tree artefact, adopted in D44. Bet at the root, initiatives below (phases), epics below that (packages), stories below that (sessions). Versioned with reasoning attached to every change. Reviewed at every phase audit.

Reasoning category taxonomy for course changes:

- **Discovery** — work surfaced something that changes scope.
- **Capacity** — operator's available time changes the throughput calculation.
- **Signal** — data from earlier work changes the priority order.
- **Hedge** — uncertainty about a future event makes optionality worth more than commitment.

The distribution of reasoning categories at phase audits is itself signal per D44. Too many capacity-driven changes mean the bet was overscoped; too many signal-driven changes mean the bet was poorly grounded; too many hedge entries mean the operator is avoiding commitment.

## Bet

A public demonstration that a senior product leader can direct the end-to-end implementation of an enterprise-grade agentic platform through AI-assisted development without writing code, with the architectural discipline holding at the level of complexity that real enterprise software requires. The platform is the artefact; the methodology that emerges is the proprietary insight. See [bet.md](bet.md) for the strategic articulation; [prfaq.md](prfaq.md) for the external articulation.

## Initiative 1: Phase 1 (in progress)

By Phase 1 close: a single tenant runs locally with the full stack; one agent can be configured, run, audited, and optimised through the platform's own tooling; the trace capture layer surfaces optimization recommendations, not just data; the methodology document captures the architect-implementer pattern with enough specificity that another senior product leader could read it and adopt the discipline. Phase 2 direction decided at the Phase 1 close audit. See [phase-1-prd.md](phase-1-prd.md) for the living phase-level PRD.

### Packages, with RICE scoring

Scoring criteria fitted to the case-study framing:

- **Reach (1–5)**: how many of the bet's evidence-needs (enterprise architectural decisions, identity federation, tenant isolation, observability differentiator, evaluation, optimization recommendations) does this package exercise.
- **Impact (1–5)**: how strongly does this package demonstrate the proposition that a senior product leader can direct enterprise-grade implementation.
- **Confidence (1–3)**: forecast confidence at the time the package was framed.
- **Effort (operator-weeks)**: estimated calendar weeks of operator time.

RICE = (R × I × C) / E. Scores are operator-judged, recorded at this strategic session as the canonical baseline; P1, P2, and P3 scores are applied retroactively. Phase 1 close audit reviews scores against actual delivery and against signal that would have changed them.

### Phase 2-A close gate — opened on the engine, not the experience (gate audit, 2026-06-09)

The Phase 2-A close gate opened at the dogfood gate audit (the strategic conversation run on the post-S73 refreshed files). The three v6 close conditions, as judged:

- **Condition 1 (a week of real use) — met on data-span, not on lived-use; lived-use binds.** The moat ran against a real, rich corpus (989 work units from the operator's actual calendar/email/task history, spanning weeks). "Real use" reads two ways, and the binding reading is lived-use — sustained daily contact — per the S58 marker ("sustained usefulness across a week of real use … not proven by a seeded demo") and D156's reverse-Kano restraint test. The S69–S72 work was a compressed build-and-read sprint (one day), not seven days of lived use. So condition 1 is met on data-span and unproven on lived-use; the lived-use validation defers to Phase 2-B's first gate (E2 below), run on a thickened signal — recorded honestly rather than claimed.
- **Condition 2 (senior-leader-ICP validation, operator-as-first-instance) — met as written (n-of-1 coherence).** The moat reads orphan work and uncovered/neglected goals on one senior leader's real life, coverage-honestly (D171), and the differentiator fired. n-of-1 validates coherence, not market fit (the Phase 2-B scale job). One finding rides it: the seeded goals skew personal (German, marathon, strength, meditation, litany); the professional-outcome spine — the ICP's actual hook — is under-tested. Phase 2-B must seed real professional goals (E1 below).
- **Condition 3 (substrate completion across the waves) — met, on the trustworthy reading.** Wave 1 (P13–P16) + Wave 2 (P17–P20) built through S68. The distinction the gate records: the moat was *built* at S68 but not *trustworthy* until S72 — going live surfaced and fixed coverage honesty (D171), recall (D172/D174), and recurrence inflation (D173/D175) in succession. Reaching trustworthy took the dogfood, which validates build-then-prove sequencing rather than undermining it. No decision silently contradicted an earlier one; the one new instrument (the coverage report) has D174 as the decision it informs.

**Verdict: the gate opens on the engine, not yet the experience.** The top-level architect-implementer bet is earned strongly (a coverage-honest, recall-tiered, recurrence-folded moat on real data, behind interfaces, on a green suite, the charter discipline holding). The Phase 2-A *product* bet is earned as an **engine, not yet a product**: the differentiator is real and fired live, but the experienced signal is thin (7 goals, 1 candidate edge each, 0 confirmed; the orphan read dominated by deliberately-unseeded life areas). The thinness is a **seeding-and-coverage problem, not an engine problem** — the single most important diagnostic the dogfood produced. The bet holds and does not shift.

### Phase 2-B opening sequence — thicken → live → clothe (v9)

Phase 2-B opens with a strict three-beat sequence, the experience layer built on what the lived-use week proves rather than on assumption:

- **E1 — thicken (by seed, not source).** Script-seed goals spanning professional and personal (the German/get-a-job precedent), to raise SERVES-edge density and shrink the orphan read so the experience layer has a dense signal to render. Thickening is by *seed*: the corpus already holds 989 units, so goal sparsity is the constraint, not source sparsity. The already-built Google Tasks pull (S65) is activated for the personal tenant — **not** the decoupled Trello package. An **opening sequence, not a numbered package**; **S74 is its first story**. A density trigger (seeded-goal coverage, professional broken out — *not* a global orphan-rate target, which the deliberately-unseeded life areas floor by design) gates entry to E2.
- **E2 — the lived-use week (a gate-milestone, not a build epic).** The lived-use validation condition 1 truly demands, run on the thickened signal, before any clothing, against a pre-committed falsifiable verdict (the reverse-Kano question: does the surface keep earning its place or curdle into guilt). Entry-gated on E1's density trigger. The week-gate definition (trigger values + verdict criteria) is **output 3 of the gate audit, ratified in v10 after S74 measures what professional coverage is achievable** — committing a possibly-unclearable trigger now is the worst outcome, so it is measured first.
- **E3 — clothe to the mockups (week-gated).** The experience layer (today surface, journal, conversation cell, dashboard, privacy, shell), mapping onto the daily-driver-surface work D156 already placed in the B-stream. Internal ordering — and its priority against the B-stream methodology packages — defers to a **post-week RICE pass**; pre-ordering now would re-commit the structure the week exists to teach.

E1 and E2 sit **ahead of P21** as the opening sequence; the B-stream is **not renumbered again** (the v8 drift-safety rules hold; the v8 old→new mapping stands unchanged). The renumbered **P21–P24 B-stream is provisionally preserved, not endorsed**: those methodology-platform packages (B9 authoring extensions, agent-runtime exercise, accumulated-history) predate the attentional-assistant pivot, and whether they survived it is an **open question resolved at the post-week RICE pass** — any re-home to Phase 3 or retirement is a deliberate later decision with its own log entry, never folded silently here.

**The seed-and-measure reframe, and a named source dependency.** S74 runs as seed-and-measure: it gates professional coverage on **confirmed (0.9) edges**, and the measurement sets the real trigger rather than asserting one. S74 Step 0 surfaced the dependency — the connected calendar's content is decisively personal (a medication regimen, family practice, social admin; no work meetings), so confirmed professional coverage cannot come from the current corpus, and **connecting the operator's work calendar is the named source dependency** before professional goals can clear. The seed-not-source rule holds for the personal/general signal; professional coverage carries this one genuine source dependency, toward the work system of record, surfaced before the week rather than during it.
