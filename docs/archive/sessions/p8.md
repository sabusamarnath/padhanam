Reflection (four prompts per the brief):

1. **Paired-NULL invariant at two pairs.** D75 introduced paired-null for methodology lineage; S26a-2 extends to role lineage as a second independent pair. The invariant structure generalises cleanly: one CHECK constraint per pair at the schema layer (`agent_templates_lineage_paired_null` for methodology, `agent_templates_role_lineage_paired_null` for role), one `__post_init__` clause per pair at the domain layer, one tenant-isolation contract test per pair at the schema-isolation layer. The three-valid-state matrix (both NULL, both populated, role-only) is small enough to enumerate in prose and in test parametrisation; the fourth combination (methodology populated, role NULL) is structurally impossible because methodology-based creation always resolves at least one role per D86 — the brief flagged this and the schema doesn't add a redundant CHECK to prevent it. A third lineage pair (e.g., skills at Phase 2) would extend the pattern to three independent pairs with three CHECK constraints, three `__post_init__` clauses, and a 2^3 valid-state matrix. The pattern accommodates a third pair without restructuring; whether the eight-state matrix remains audit-friendly is the open question. The complexity ceiling is not at three pairs but at the point where the cross-pair relationships become non-independent (e.g., if a skills pair could only populate when a role pair is populated, that's a tightening invariant requiring its own CHECK and its own `__post_init__` clause). For now, the pattern is robust at two pairs and remains compositional.

2. **Cross-context ports at second consumer.** Agent context now hosts two cross-context lookup ports: `MethodologyLookup` (D79, S25) and `RoleLookup` (S26a-2). The structural duplication is visible but not yet costly: a shared "cross-context-lookup pattern" abstraction would compress two adapters into one but at the cost of obscuring per-port differences (single-hop role lookup vs join-resolution methodology lookup). A third port with the same shape — single-hop, version-resolving, consumer-DTO-shaped — would tip the balance toward abstraction. Promotion candidate for the Patterns-observed list if a third such port lands.
