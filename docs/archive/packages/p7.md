## Goal

Agent authoring substrate. P7 ships the platform's ability to
store, version, and manipulate agent specifications across two
new bounded contexts (methodology + agent), with methodology
templates serving as platform-managed defaults that tenants
clone into independent agent instances per D68's clones-are-
independent commitment. By P7 close, the operator can author
a methodology template through the CLI, ingest sources for a
tenant, clone an agent from the methodology with those sources
attached, and edit the agent independently of the methodology
template that produced it — exercising the cross-context flow
P8's runtime will invoke against and P5's eval harness will
score against in subsequent phases.

## Deltas against epic note v1

The v1 epic note forecast three to five sessions and four to five
D-entries beyond D68. Actual: three build sessions plus three
mid-package strategic commits; six P7-era D-entries beyond D68
plus five mid-package-strategic D-entries (D69-D78 = ten D-entries
shipped across the P7 window). Six discrete deviations vs the v1
epic note:

1. **Three build sessions versus the forecast's three-to-five.**
   The lower bound of the forecast held. S25 closed P7 directly
   without spinning S26 because all 13 substantive acceptance
   criteria were met at S25 close and the P6/S22 precedent
   applied cleanly (close-or-spin decision deferred to session-
   time per D43's session-by-session framing discipline). The
   epic note's "Three to four sessions" lower bound is the
   structurally-honest shape; the conservative four-to-five
   upper bound priced in the cross-context complexity that S25
   absorbed cleanly.

2. **Ten P7-era D-entries versus the forecast's four to five.**
   D74 (methodology aggregate), D75 (agent aggregate), D79
   (cross-context flow shape) are the three build-session D-
   entries the epic note anticipated. The remaining seven
   (D69-D73 security-readiness; D76-D78 consumer-direction
   placement) belong to the two mid-package strategic blocks
   the v1 epic note did not anticipate. Strategic-mode D-entries
   between build sessions is a P6-precedented pattern (Ask David
   absorption; product reframe) recurring at P7 — five D-entries
   across two mid-package strategic blocks is consistent with
   the strategic-mode reflection-density discipline from D47.

3. **Two mid-package strategic blocks landed; epic note v1
   anticipated none.** The security-readiness gap closure (five
   D-entries plus the Layer A scaffold) and the consumer-direction
   placement (three D-entries plus five deferred-decisions
   entries) both surfaced from contextual pressure during P7
   build (security-readiness from S23/S24 framing review;
   consumer-direction from OpenClaw analysis post-S24 close).
   The mid-package-strategic-commit pattern continues from P6
   (two blocks: Ask David, product reframe) into P7 (two blocks:
   security-readiness, consumer-direction) — a steady-state
   characteristic of phase work rather than an aberration.

4. **Hash-chain helper promotion mechanic absorbed at S24.**
   The v1 epic note did not anticipate the cross-context import
   prohibition surfacing as a constraint on the hash-chain
   helper at the second-consumer moment. S24's commit 8 absorbed
   the promotion as a build-time mechanic (the helper moves from
   `contexts/methodology/domain/hash_chain.py` to
   `padhanam/security/hash_chain.py` with a field-set-agnostic
   API), refining the second-consumer-promotes pattern with the
   sub-observation that helper-API shape settles at the second-
   consumer moment. The refinement is documented at D75 alternative
   (h)'s rejection reasoning.

5. **Application-layer ports subpackage emerged at S25.** The v1
   epic note did not anticipate a `contexts/agent/application/
   ports/` subpackage distinct from the existing
   `contexts/agent/ports/`. D79's consumer-side abstractions
   (MethodologyLookup, SourceLookup, MethodologyView,
   SourceNotFoundError) needed a structural home distinct from
   the persistence-side AgentRepositoryPort; placing them at
   `contexts/agent/application/ports/` rather than mixing them

   structurally honest shape because they are consumer-side
   Protocol ports at the application layer, not persistence-port
   abstractions. The split lands the subpackage with two ports

   subpackage convention.
